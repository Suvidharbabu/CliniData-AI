Agentic AI

Specialist agents, human decisions

A planning orchestrator splits each compliance question into sub-tasks for specialist agents. Every step is logged, and no CRITICAL or HIGH finding leads to action without a named human approver.

Walkthrough

"Show all HIPAA §164.312 gaps"

What happens between the question and the approval queue.

  1. OrchestratorScopes the question to HIPAA §164.312 and selects instructions such as "Addressable is not optional" and "Encryption coverage by device class".
  2. RetrieverRuns HyDE, dense and sparse retrieval, RRF and reranking to pick the top 10 obligation, control and policy chunks.
  3. Graph traversalPulls every §164.312 obligation, their controls, evidence and risks, plus mapped Part 11 and ISO 27001 obligations.
  4. Gap MapperAutomatic logoff has no control, and laptop encryption is only partly rolled out.
  5. Constraint Verifier & Cross-ReferenceChecks deadlines and compound gaps across the mapped frameworks.
  6. Risk ScorerScores residual risk for unattended clinic workstations and lost unencrypted laptops.
  7. Reporter & guardClaude writes the cited analysis, and any citation that is not in the subgraph is flagged.
  8. Approval gateHIGH gaps go to a senior compliance manager and CRITICAL gaps to the CCO, with a 3-day deadline and escalation.
Governance

Human-in-the-loop controls

Mandatory approval

CRITICAL and HIGH gaps need human approval before any remediation runs, and the check is enforced in the service layer.

Full context for approvers

Each request shows the gap summary, AI reasoning, confidence, risk level and recommended remediation, with a 3-day deadline.

Immutable decisions

Approver identity, role, timestamp, comments and AI confidence are logged in an append-only audit trail.

Automatic escalation

Overdue requests escalate to the next role level, ending with the Chief Compliance Officer.

Replayable traces

Every graph query, prompt version, model, response hash and intermediate output is stored with the run.

Role separation

Compliance officers ask, compliance managers and the CCO decide, and auditors read, as enforced by RBAC.
