Roadmap

Twelve months to production

Four phases, starting with the core clinical frameworks and scaling out to the full regulatory portfolio of a life-sciences enterprise.

Months 1–3

Phase 1: Foundation

  • Infrastructure: PostgreSQL, Neo4j, Pinecone, sparse search, queues, API skeleton.
  • User & access management: RBAC, JWT, MFA, SSO.
  • Ingestion pipeline: OCR, clause-aware chunking, NER, graph loader, dual embeddings.
  • Initial graph for HIPAA, GDPR (health data) and 21 CFR Part 11.
  • Basic hybrid retrieval: HyDE, dense + sparse, RRF.
Months 4–6

Phase 2: Core intelligence

  • Adaptive instruction codebook with semantic routing and EMA feedback.
  • Control registry and risk register.
  • Orchestrator plus Gap Mapper, Constraint Verifier, Cross-Reference and Risk Scorer agents.
  • Cross-encoder reranker fine-tuned on clinical compliance document pairs.
  • Compliance dashboard with graph visualisation; Audit Tracer and evidence bundles.
  • APM, metrics and structured agent tracing.
Months 7–9

Phase 3: Enterprise features

  • Human approval module with deadlines, escalation and immutable decision logs.
  • Remediation Planner and remediation workflow.
  • Regulatory change monitor with the Change Impact Agent (FDA, EMA, HHS, MeitY feeds).
  • Alerting through Jira, Slack and email; async reporting.
  • Multi-tenant isolation, data residency and encryption at rest.
  • Penetration testing, security audit, SOC 2 Type II readiness.
Months 10–12

Phase 4: Scale & production

  • Read replicas for Postgres and Neo4j.
  • Queue-lag-based autoscaling and GPU workers for embeddings.
  • Library extended to ICH E6(R3), ICH E2A/E2B, EU CTR 536/2014, DPDP Act, CDSCO and ISO 27001.
  • CI/CD maturity, incident playbooks, DR rehearsal, go-live.
